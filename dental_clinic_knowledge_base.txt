## 7. PREPARATION & POST-PROCEDURE CARE INSTRUCTIONS
- Before Dental Surgery / Implant: Eat a light meal before your visit; inform the doctor if you are taking blood thinners or diabetes medication.
- After Tooth Extraction / Root Canal:
  * Bite firmly on the sterile gauze pack for 45 minutes.
  * Do not spit, smoke, or use a straw for 24 hours to prevent dry socket.
  * Consume only soft, cold, or room-temperature foods (ice cream, yogurt, khichdi, smoothies) for the first 24 hours.
  * Apply an ice pack on the outside of your cheek if mild swelling occurs.
  * Start gentle warm saltwater rinses from the next day.

## 8. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQS)
- Q: Does Root Canal Treatment (RCT) hurt?
  A: No, our clinic uses painless computer-controlled local anesthesia (The Wand) and rotary microscopes. The procedure is virtually painless.
- Q: How long does a dental implant procedure take?
  A: The implant placement takes only 30 to 45 minutes per tooth under local anesthesia with same-day temporary crowns available.
- Q: How often should I get teeth cleaning done?
  A: The American Dental Association recommends professional dental scaling and cleaning every 6 months to prevent cavities and gum disease.
- Q: Are X-rays safe during pregnancy?
  A: We use ultra-low radiation digital sensors and lead protective aprons. Non-urgent procedures are scheduled during the second trimester.
- Q: Do you provide treatment for children?
